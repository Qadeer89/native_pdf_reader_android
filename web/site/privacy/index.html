Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the PDF Reader Android app ("the App," package com.pdfreader.app) handles your information. The App is local-first: opening, editing, annotating, and signing PDF files happens entirely on your device, with no account and no internet connection required.

Data the App never collects

The App does not read, scan, or transmit the contents of your PDF files to us or to any third party. There is no account sign-in and no cloud storage of your documents in the current version of the App. The App does not use analytics SDKs beyond what Google's own AdMob service reports (see below). We do not sell your data.

Cloud sync — not currently available

A future version of the App may offer an optional "Sign in with Google" and cloud sync feature, letting you back up chosen documents to your own Google account. That feature is not enabled in the current version — there is no sign-in option in the App, and no document data is sent anywhere. If this feature is enabled in a future release, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is collected and why, and signing in will always remain optional.

Advertising

The App shows a banner advertisement at the bottom of the reading screen, served by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where required (for example, under GDPR/UK rules), and respects your choice. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and other device/usage information to select and measure ads; see Google's advertising policy for details of what Google collects and how it's used. No PDF content or document data is ever shared with the advertising SDK.

Device permissions

The App requests access to files/documents you explicitly open via the Android system file picker, and internet access (needed only for ads). The App does not request broad storage access, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Local-only data

Saved signatures, signing identities, and recent-document history are stored only on your device, in app-private storage. Nothing is backed up or sent anywhere.

Data deletion

You can delete a signing identity or saved signature at any time from within the App.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App's features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the App's Google Play Store page.
