PDF Reader

A fast, local-first PDF reader for Android — read, annotate, sign, and manage your PDFs, with your files staying on your device by default.

Read & navigate

Smooth zoom, continuous or single-page scrolling, bookmarks, search, and full-screen reading.

Annotate & edit

Highlights, notes, page rotate/move/merge/extract, and form filling — all without leaving the app.

Sign documents

Draw, type, or import a signature, or apply a real digital (cryptographic) signature to a document.

Private by default

Your documents stay on your device. Signing in and cloud sync are optional, and off unless you turn them on.
