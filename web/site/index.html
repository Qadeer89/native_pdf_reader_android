What it does

Everything a PDF needs, in one reader

No bloat, no forced sign-in, no file upload to a third-party server to "process" your document.

Read & navigate

Smooth zoom, continuous or single-page scrolling, bookmarks, full-text search, and a distraction-free full-screen mode.

Annotate & edit

Highlights and sticky notes, plus page rotate, move, merge, and extract — and fill out form fields in place.

Sign documents

Draw, type, or import a signature for everyday use, or apply a real certificate-based digital signature when it matters.

Private by default

Documents stay on your device. There's no account and no cloud storage — nothing to opt out of.
